GDPR Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice is for residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. It supplements our general Privacy Policy and describes how Yuto Miyashita ("Service Provider") processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your information is:
Yuto Miyashita
Email: syoken.dev@gmail.com

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: Where you have given clear consent for us to process your personal data for a specific purpose (e.g., personalized advertising via AdMob).
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our application, analyze usage via Firebase, and ensure the security of our services.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Information We Collect and Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that collect data to help us provide and improve the Application:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements. It may collect device identifiers and location data for personalized ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to understand how users interact with the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports to improve app stability.

For more details, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights under GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of your data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can request that we delete your data via the "Delete Account" button in the app or by contacting us.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we stop processing your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time via the app settings.

5. Data Transfer Outside the EEA

Since the Service Provider is located in Japan, your data may be transferred to and processed in Japan. Japan has been recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection (Adequacy Decision).

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service or as required by law. Anonymous or aggregated data may be stored indefinitely.

7. Contact

To exercise your rights or if you have any questions, please contact us at: syoken.dev@gmail.com.
